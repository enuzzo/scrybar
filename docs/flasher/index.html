ScryBar Firmware Flasher

Flash ScryBar firmware directly from your browser via USB. No tools to install. Pick a variant, plug in, click flash.

Full

recommended

Everything included: word clock, weather, RSS reader, Wikipedia deck, and DOOM.

Lite

smaller

Word clock, weather, RSS reader, Wikipedia deck. No DOOM — smaller flash footprint.

After flashing

  1. Device boots and starts a WiFi access point (ScryBar-Setup-XXXX)
  2. Connect to it from your phone or laptop
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1:8080 to configure WiFi, theme, language, weather location, and more

Requires a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera) with Web Serial support. Safari and Firefox are not supported.
