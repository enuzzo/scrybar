# ScryBar v0.9.1

Firmware **DB-M0-r301** · Companion **v0.3.2 (37)** · 2 September 2026

## Highlights

- Makes the Bambu printer serial the primary saved identity and treats its LAN IP as refreshable discovery state.
- Automatically migrates a configured printer to its current DHCP address and reconnects local MQTT without requiring a manual refresh.
- Rejects identity-free SSDP echoes that can look like a Bambu printer but do not expose a serial or model.
- Keeps verified LAN-service discovery and manual-IP fallback available for printers that do not advertise over multicast.

## Downloads

- **Web installer:** flash firmware r301 directly from the ScryBar website with a Chromium-based browser.
- **ScryBar Companion 0.3.2:** universal macOS DMG for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
- **Firmware binaries:** attached for manual installation and also published with the web installer.

## Notes

- The Bambu LAN access code stays in macOS Keychain and is never sent to ScryBar.
- The Companion DMG is ad-hoc signed for this community release and is not Apple-notarized.
- See `CHANGELOG.md` for the full technical change list.
